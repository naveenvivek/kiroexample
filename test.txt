Layer 4: Guardrails & Governance
Every decision the AI makes gets logged, explained, and constrained. This is non-negotiable in banking.
	∙	Audit trail of every decision
	∙	Confidence scores on every output
	∙	Human-in-the-loop triggers when confidence is low
	∙	Explainability — why did the AI make this decision?
	∙	Regulatory compliance checks (EU AI Act, DORA)
Layer 5: Orchestration Interface
This is how blocks connect to each other to form workflows. One block’s output becomes another block’s input.

Payment File Received
    → [Format Detection Block]
    → [Format Conversion Block]
    → [Sanctions Screening Block]
    → [Fraud Detection Block]
    → [Reconciliation Block]
    → Payment Processed

The Business Model
There are three ways to deliver reusable AI blocks:
1. Platform Model (Backbase, Temenos approach) — You build a platform with a library of blocks. Banks subscribe and configure. Highest revenue, hardest to build.
2. Accelerator Model (Neurons Lab approach) — Pre-built blocks that consultants deploy and customize at each bank. Faster to market, services-heavy.
3. Open Core Model — Open-source the block framework, sell enterprise features (governance, compliance, support). Good for adoption, harder to monetize.

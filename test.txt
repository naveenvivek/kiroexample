Layer 4: Guardrails & Governance
Every decision the AI makes gets logged, explained, and constrained. This is non-negotiable in banking.
	∙	Audit trail of every decision
	∙	Confidence scores on every output
	∙	Human-in-the-loop triggers when confidence is low
	∙	Explainability — why did the AI make this decision?
	∙	Regulatory compliance checks (EU AI Act, DORA)
